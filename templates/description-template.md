# Description template for short-form videos

Copy this structure for every upload. The rules come first; the template is at the bottom.

## Rules

1. **The first two lines are the only ones anyone reads.** They appear above the "...more" fold
   and double as the search snippet. Say what the video gives the viewer, in the words the
   viewer would have typed into search.
2. **Run the query test before publishing.** Write down the three search queries this video
   should win. If the title and the first two description lines do not contain the words in
   those queries, fix the title — not the description.
3. **Tags are for disambiguation, not stuffing.** Use the spellings, names and abbreviations
   people actually type. Around 15 is plenty; an unrelated tag wall is against platform terms
   and does not rank you.
4. **Hashtags: a few, specific, on-topic.** Three to five that name the subject and the niche.
   Generic reach-bait tags (#viral, #fyp) describe nothing and help no one find the video later.

## Template

```text
<Line 1: what happens in the video, with the search words in it. One or two sentences.>
<Line 2: why it is worth watching to the end, or what the payoff is.>

<Optional: the longer version — what is covered, who it is for, any links or resources.>

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

## Worked example

```text
A giant tap is gushing over a tiny kitchen — watch the tiny crew seal the leak with a
single red rubber band.
Watch till the end for the celebration when the flood finally stops.

#miniature #satisfying #tinyworkers
```
